## Lost in the first round: another language won the group — 587 file(s)

**Mechanism.** The options are asked in 4 groups of ~200 (a question takes at most 255); in the group holding the expected language, the model picks another one. For Kev, mostly an option listed near the start of the group (a primacy bias over long option lists — see analysis/KEV-VS-JEV.md).

**Verified.** the expected language's group sent another winner to the final.

**Suggested fix.** shorter option lists (smaller groups, or the extension's candidates first); for Kev, several orders.
